# S01 — Drawing Contract

- Status: `WAIT_USER_INPUT`
- Dependency: M01 accepted
- Allowed paths: `docs/prd/**`, future `config/drawing-contract.*`

## Questions to resolve

1. Exact drawing type and intended use.
2. Units, scale/not-to-scale rule, sheet size and orientation.
3. Company layer/text/plot standards.
4. Approved pump tags/types and required detail level.
5. Approved valve and instrument symbols/standards.
6. Suction/discharge routes and flow directions.
7. Line sizes, pressure ratings, material classes, insulation/heat tracing.
8. Seal-plan and instrument callouts.
9. Title-block data, drawing number, revision and language.
10. Whether screenshot likeness or engineering clarity has priority.

## Acceptance

Every required field has a source or explicit owner decision. Contradictions are resolved in writing.

## Stop

Any unresolved safety/specification field produces `HOLD_ENGINEERING_CONFIRMATION`.
